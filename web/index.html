Architecture

Six agents.
One unified intelligence.

Every query triggers a choreographed pipeline - each agent playing a precise role, self-correcting until confident.

01🔀
Router
Intercepts casual queries for instant answers. Detects tone - formal, friendly, technical, ELI5 - from your words.
gpt-oss-20b
02📋
Planner
Classifies intent into 10 research types. Breaks complex queries into 4-5 targeted sub-tasks for full coverage.
gpt-oss-20b
03⚡
Researcher
Fires parallel Tavily web searches via ThreadPoolExecutor. Cuts research time from ~60s to under 10s per pass.
Tavily API
04🧠
Retriever
Embeds all results with SentenceTransformers 384-dim vectors. Retrieves only the most relevant chunks from Qdrant.
Qdrant + SentenceTransformers
05🧐
Critic
Hybrid scorer: 60% LLM judgment + 40% objective signals. Loops back if confidence is low - up to 3 times.
Self-Correcting
06📝
Reporter
Writes a fully formatted markdown report, dynamically adapting tone and depth to match your original intent.
Tone-Aware
